Technology serves as the means, never as the centerpiece of the experience. There is demand for this. A study by Itaú in partnership with Grupo Consumoteca shows that 80% of Brazilians want to better organize their financial lives and seek more effective support for decisions, and that 65% would like technologies that act as a financial copilot, providing ongoing guidance. “OpenAI’s models were very important in delivering this experience, because they were able to operate across this range of solutions.” —Carlos Eduardo Mazzei, CTO, Itaú ia.i: from convenience to advice, in one place The journey began in 2024, when the bank introduced the first Inteligência Itaú capabilities for specific financial needs. ia.i is the next stage: a complete conversational layer, activated by text or voice, bringing interactions related to checking accounts, credit, cards, insurance, and other services into one environment — and connecting customers to specialized human support whenever needed.
